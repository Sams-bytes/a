    Welcome to Aegis

    Add a domain on the left. You prove you own it with a DNS record, then Aegis checks what the internet can see: open ports, encryption and web settings. You get a score and a plain-English list of what to fix.

    1. Add your domain
    2. Verify it with a DNS TXT record
    3. Scan it and fix the findings, worst first

    Aegis only scans domains you have verified. Scanning systems you don't own without permission is a crime in the EU.
